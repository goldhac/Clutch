When to interrupt

A modal costs the user their place

Use one only when the next step needs a decision — money, deletion, or a failure the user has to know about. Progress and success go to toasts.

Say what it costs, in the modal

Prices, refunds and what happens to a credit are stated on the card, not one click away. No modal ever hides the free path.

Dismissal is always free

Scrim click, Escape and a named secondary action all leave the state untouched. Nothing is charged or deleted by closing a card.
