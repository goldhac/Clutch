01 — PHILOSOPHY

Design for 2am, 48 hours out, stress at its peak.

Every surface answers one question and offers one obvious next action. The polish comes from restraint — spacing, hierarchy, and type quality — not from decoration. This should feel like a professional tool a student is happy to pay for because it visibly respects their time and their intelligence.

The moat is trust. When the sheet claims a topic is likely tested, it shows a confidence dot, a ★ if the pattern is verified against a real past exam, and a citation back to the source. That evidence layer is never hidden for the sake of a cleaner-looking page — it is the product.

CONFIDENCE INTERVALS
★Mean, σ unknown
x̄ ± t*(n−1) · (s / √n)
Use t when σ unknown (almost always). df = n−1.
Mean, σ known
x̄ ± z* · (σ / √n)
Proportion
p̂ ± z* · √(p̂(1−p̂)/n)
z*: 90%→1.645 · 95%→1.96 · 99%→2.576
L08 s21–24 · Final '19 Q3, '21 Q2
z vs t — WHICH TEST? ★
{{ z.cond }}
{{ z.use }}
Rule: σ known → z. σ unknown → t (df=n−1). n≥30 → t≈z.
REGRESSION & CORRELATION
Least-squares line
ŷ = b₀ + b₁x · b₁ = r(s_y/s_x)
r ∈ [−1,1] measures linear strength; r² = variance explained.
L13 s2–7
HYPOTHESIS TESTING ★
1. State H₀ (=) and Hₐ (<, >, ≠).
2. Pick α (0.05 default).
3. Test stat t = (x̄−μ₀)/(s/√n)
4. Find p-value from the tail(s).
5. p < α → reject H₀.
L10 s3–9 · every past final
p-VALUE & ERROR TYPES
p-value
P(data this extreme | H₀ true). Not P(H₀ true). Small p = evidence against H₀.
Type I (α)
Reject true H₀ · false positive
Type II (β)
Keep false H₀ · false negative
Power = 1 − β
L11 s6–14

SE = σ/√n
CLT: for n ≥ 30, x̄ ≈ Normal regardless of population shape.

WORKED EXAMPLE — 95% CI
n=40, x̄=52, s=8, 95% → t*39≈2.02
SE = 8/√40 = 1.26
ME = 2.02 × 1.26 = 2.55
CI = 52 ± 2.55 = (49.5, 54.5)
"We\u2019re 95% confident the true mean is between 49.5 and 54.5." Since 50 is inside, don\u2019t reject H₀: μ=50.
